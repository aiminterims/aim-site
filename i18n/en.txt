299|My expertise
300|Systematizing and structuring the business.
301|We will examine how the company is set up: who is responsible for what, where functions overlap and where nobody is responsible. We will build an organizational structure that works without your daily involvement and separate the roles of owner and manager.
302|Finding internal profit reserves.
303|We will find money inside the business without investing new funds: losses in processes, non-core expenses, ineffective products and customers, weak points in pricing. We will draw up a prioritized list of specific steps.
304|Life and business priorities.
305|We will work out what in your life and business really matters now and what takes your time. We will define what you are ready to spend resources on, what to give up, and how to combine the executive role with personal life.
306|Strategy.
307|We will formulate where you are taking the business over 3–5 years, which decisions need to be made now and which risks to build in. We will turn the strategy into a clear plan with stages and a measure of success.
