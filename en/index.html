How we work

Conversation

30 minutes with an AIM partner: the task, timing, budget, expected result.

Selection in 72 hours

We offer 2–3 candidates from the AIM pool who have already solved exactly such tasks.

Start and diagnostics

The interim starts on the project, runs a diagnostic in the first weeks, and records the figures and a plan with KPIs.

Result and handover

The task is solved — the interim prepares a successor and hands over.
