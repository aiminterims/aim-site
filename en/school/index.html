School of Interim Managers

You spent 20 years building someone else's business. Time to build your own profession

The School is for you if you

  • Have outgrown employment and keep hearing “overqualified”
  • Are a manager 45+ who has hit the salary ceiling
  • Turned losses into profit, launched business lines, cut costs
  • Are a consultant who knows: strategy without implementation is worth nothing
  • Are on a career break and want to return with a new status
  • Are a functional expert ready to run a whole business

In 7 weeks you will have

  • Interim packagingA CV and offer in the “problem → action → result” format
  • PositioningClear expertise that an owner pays for
  • Entry protocolWhat to do in the first 30 days on a project
  • Defended contractAuthority, KPIs, fee and exit from the project
  • AIM certificate and poolAfter defending your case — access to the Association's closed projects

Program: from strategy to your first project

  1. Introduction and overview of the program
  2. Leadership in non-standard situations
  3. From employment to interim: how to repackage yourself
  4. Expertise and positioning
  5. CV, platforms, reaching employers
  6. First 30 days on a project
  7. Executive search: how to strengthen your team
  8. Strategy into action and KPIs: the language of money with the owner
  9. Digitalization and technology in an interim's work
  10. Change Management
  11. Team and trust from day one
  12. Legal basics of the interim contract and handover
  13. Sales and marketing: a project pipeline for 5+ years
  14. Exam: defending your own case
