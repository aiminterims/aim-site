Personal mentoring for a business owner or executive

I am an experienced executive, so I value my time and respect yours. Before you come to me for a consultation, let us decide whether I am the right mentor for you.

My expertise

  • Systematizing and structuring the business.We will examine how the company is set up: who is responsible for what, where functions overlap and where nobody is responsible. We will build an organizational structure that works without your daily involvement and separate the roles of owner and manager.
  • Finding internal profit reserves.We will find money inside the business without investing new funds: losses in processes, non-core expenses, ineffective products and customers, weak points in pricing. We will draw up a prioritized list of specific steps.
  • Life and business priorities.We will work out what in your life and business really matters now and what takes your time. We will define what you are ready to spend resources on, what to give up, and how to combine the executive role with personal life.
  • Strategy.We will formulate where you are taking the business over 3–5 years, which decisions need to be made now and which risks to build in. We will turn the strategy into a clear plan with stages and a measure of success.

I am set for serious work. So I definitely cannot help those interested in active sales (especially to me), choosing a life partner or fortune-telling.

Working with me involves

  • A program of 10 sessions.We will discuss the current situation and formulate the main problems you want to solve. We will outline ways to solve them.
  • An individual development trajectory.We will form a plan for your development in the near future. Following it, you will rethink your attitude to existing processes and start changing them.
  • Your independent work between sessions.Between sessions you will follow recommendations and stick to the planned course. Otherwise our sessions make no sense.
  • A long-term support program.I offer sessions every two weeks to discuss what you have managed to achieve and adjust further actions.
